## What it is

This is Fulfen Labs' knowledge base on forward deployed engineering: the work of building, shipping and handing over
AI systems inside a customer's organisation.
Fulfen Labs is an illustrative practice built around *The Forward Deployed Engineer's Handbook* by Daniel Vaughan.
It shows how such a practice runs, and it does not offer services.

The knowledge base is new.
This page is its first entry, and the articles follow once each has been reviewed.

## How an article is made

1. Research and drafting happen in a private repository.
   Web research uses general topic searches, and every fact kept carries its source, its publisher and its date.
2. A draft becomes an article only when Daniel Vaughan has reviewed it and said yes.
3. The article is then copied to this site automatically, with its list of sources and the date it was last reviewed.

Each article carries Daniel Vaughan's name and says how it was made: researched and drafted with AI tools, then
reviewed and edited by him, with the date its sources were last checked.
"We" in an article means Fulfen Labs.

## What every article promises

- Every claim is one of three kinds: a sourced fact with its date, the author's own experience said as his, or
  opinion labelled as opinion.
- Claims about AI tools and models carry absolute dates, because the tools change month by month.
- No employer or client is named.
- Articles are vendor-neutral: a vendor is named only to describe a published source or a comparison.

## What is never published here

- Research notes and working drafts.
- Transcripts of talks and videos.
  They are other people's words, and they stay in the private research repository.
